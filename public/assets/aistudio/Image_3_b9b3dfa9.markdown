## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the ethos of a high-end botanical nursery and artisanal plant atelier. It merges contemporary editorial elegance with organic warmth, steering clear of rustic clichés in favor of architectural purity, structured typography, and tranquil, nature-infused whitespaces.

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with understated tactile nuances:
- **Atmosphere:** Serene, cultivated, grounded, and modern.
- **Audience:** Discerning interior decorators, plant collectors, biophilic design enthusiasts, and mindful urban gardeners looking for curated flora and white-glove plant care.
- **Emotional Response:** Induces immediate visual calm, evoking the tactile sensory experience of stone planters, damp potting soil, and sunlit conservatories.
- **Visual Tenets:** Generous, airy structural framing; high-contrast editorial hierarchy; ultra-refined border lines; and focused, purposeful color pops for conversational commerce.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from living foliage, terracotta pottery, and natural linen fibers, balanced by functional high-utility accents:

- **Primary Botanical Deep Green (`#2E5A44`):** Anchors brand authority. Used for primary interactive triggers, active navigation markers, key headers, and dominant badges.
- **Secondary Leaf Sage (`#4A7C59`):** Soft companion green for hover states, tonal highlight tiles, and contextual category cues.
- **Tertiary Earthy Terracotta (`#D97757`):** An organic, fired-clay accent allocated exclusively to critical callouts, sale notifications, limited seasonal drops, and subtle notification indicators.
- **Canvas Linen / Cream (`#F9F8F6`):** The foundational global background setting an organic, warm tone, avoiding sterile bluish whites.
- **Card Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity focal surface used for elevated elements, product showcases, modals, and sheets.
- **Delicate Border Tone (`#E8E5DF`):** Barely-there structural line work delineating layout grids and containers without visually cluttering the viewport.
- **Dark Charcoal Slate (`#222222`):** Primary text color delivering WCAG AAA contrast while remaining softer than pure `#000000`.
- **Botanical Olive Slate (`#5A685F`):** Secondary text and muted metadata color (latin botanical names, care guides, microcopy).
- **Direct Order Green (`#25D366`):** Specific functional accent reserved strictly for direct WhatsApp ordering and availability pings.

## Typography

The typographic strategy balances the sculptural modernity of **Outfit** with the functional precision of **Inter**:

- **Display & Headlines (Outfit):** Delivers an architectural, upscale aesthetic. Generous round bowls and balanced geometry mimic organic growth patterns while preserving crisp readability. Display styles use relaxed medium-to-regular weights to avoid harsh visual mass.
- **Botanical Body & Reading (Inter):** Highly neutral and legible down to micro-sizes. Used for narrative care specifications, dimensions, shipping details, and inputs.
- **Editorial Eyebrows (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with +12% letter spacing. Used for botanical family classifications, nursery provenance, status tags, and metadata overviews.
- **Latin Binomials:** Scientific names (e.g., *Monstera deliciosa*) must consistently be set in `body-sm` or `body-md` with `font-style: italic` and `color: #5A685F`.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around deliberate whitespace that treats digital layouts like pages of an upscale botanical monograph:

- **Grid Framework:** A 12-column fluid grid system on desktop (`max-width: 1440px`), collapsing to 6 columns on tablet (breakpoint: 768px - 1024px), and 2 or 4 columns on mobile (< 768px).
- **Rhythm & Hierarchy:**
  - `margin` establishes airy borders along viewport boundaries, keeping interfaces uncluttered.
  - `space-xl` separates distinct narrative blocks (e.g., separating product showcases from accordion FAQs).
  - `space-md` is the canonical spacer for internal container padding, keeping forms and card elements compact yet readable.
- **Reflow Specifications:** Desktop layouts feature asymmetric pairings (e.g., large single editorial plant portrait spanning 7 columns with 5 columns for purchase options). On mobile screens, modules linearize cleanly with sticky bottom ordering bars.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic digital dropshadows, favoring organic planes, crisp hairline borders, and subtle diffuse environmental shading:

- **Plane Differentiation:** Natural depth is primarily achieved by nesting crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) modules atop the warm linen background (`#F9F8F6`).
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Hairline strokes (`1px solid #E8E5DF`) structure cards, dividers, modal headers, and table cells.
- **Ambient Flora Shadow:** Reserved strictly for hovering interactive elements (e.g., product cards, active dropdowns, floating drawers). Character: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(46, 90, 68, 0.08)`. The deep green tint softly grounds the elevated component rather than relying on muddy grey carbon shadows.
- **Backdrop Overlays:** Modal screens and product inspection drawers use a soft tinted veil: `rgba(34, 34, 34, 0.35)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern radii that soften architectural lines without appearing juvenile:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Applied to standard interactive touchpoints, card containers, form fields, and dropdown panels.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / `16px`):** Applied to primary product imagery frames, feature showcase blocks, admin drawers, and dialog modals.
- **Pill Treatment (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for status chips, category filters, price tags, and direct WhatsApp contact pills.
- **Media Containers:** Image boundaries maintain `overflow: hidden` to allow smooth internal scale transitions on hover without breaching border boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#2E5A44`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Smooth hover to `#4A7C59`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #2E5A44`, text `#2E5A44`. Hover transforms to background `#2E5A44` with text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Direct-Order WhatsApp CTA:** Background `#25D366`, text `#FFFFFF`, icon 18px WhatsApp glyph aligned left with 8px margin. Hover subtly darkens to `#20BA5A` with shadow `0 6px 16px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.25)`.
- **Tertiary Link:** Text `#2E5A44`, font weight 600, with a bottom 1px line animated on hover.

### Badges & Pill Chips
- **Status & Attribute Badges:** Height `24px`, padding `0 10px`, radius `9999px`, typography `label-caps`.
  - *Light Requirements:* Background `#F9F8F6`, border `1px solid #E8E5DF`, text `#5A685F`.
  - *Seasonal / Rare:* Background `rgba(217, 119, 87, 0.12)`, border `none`, text `#D97757`.
  - *WhatsApp Quick Connect:* Background `rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.12)`, text `#1EA952`.

### Product Cards
- Card base: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E5DF`, radius `1rem`, padding `1rem`.
- Image aspect ratio: `4:5` portrait crop with subtle rounded corners (`0.5rem`).
- Hover dynamic: Image scales to `1.04` over `400ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` with subtle ambient flora shadow beneath the card frame.
- Content stack: Upper right floating price pill, botanical name in `headline-sm`, latin classification in italicized `body-sm`, followed by light/water requirement micro-icons and full-width WhatsApp direct order button at bottom.

### Service Feature Cards
- Minimalist modules with background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E5DF`, padding `2rem 1.5rem`.
- Icons: Clean, 24px mono-line SVG illustrations in `#2E5A44` (e.g., watering can, delivery van, botanical shears, potting soil bag) placed inside a `44px` circular linen background (`#F9F8F6`).

### Interactive Accordion FAQs
- Divider-driven layout without container cards (`border-bottom: 1px solid #E8E5DF`).
- Trigger: Typography `headline-sm` with a rotating geometric `+` / `—` indicator in `#5A685F`.
- Body: Typography `body-md`, color `#5A685F`, padding `0.75rem 0 1.5rem 0`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Text inputs & selects: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E5DF`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`, typography `body-md`.
- Focus state: Border color `#2E5A44`, outline `2px solid rgba(46, 90, 68, 0.15)`.
- Placeholder: Color `#5A685F` at 60% opacity.

### Admin Product Management Drawer
- Slide-over panel positioned right, max-width `540px`, background `#FFFFFF`, full viewport height, border-left `1px solid #E8E5DF`.
- Header: Sticky top bar with `headline-md`, close icon button, and secondary actions.
- Form layout: Dense `space-md` vertical stack with dropzone image uploader (dashed `1.5px` border in `#E8E5DF`, linen hover background), stock counters, botanical category selectors, and sticky footer containing "Save Specimen" action button.
